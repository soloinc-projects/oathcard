Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-17

We Collect Zero Data

Oathcard is built to protect your privacy by design. The app collects, transmits, stores, and shares zero personal data.

What Happens When You Use The App

No Internet Connection Required: Oathcard works completely offline. The app makes zero network calls. No data leaves your device.

No Accounts: You don't create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to use the app.

No Tracking: The app contains no analytics, crash reporting, advertising networks, or telemetry.

No Server Storage: We have no servers that store or process your app usage, quiz results, or any other data about you.

What Data Is In The App

Oathcard includes two static datasets bundled directly in the app:

  • 2025 USCIS Civics Bank: The official 128-question civics interview bank, transcribed from the USCIS M-1778 document. This is public government content.
  • State Officials Lookup: Current names and titles of governors and senators for all 50 states, D.C., and 5 U.S. territories. Sourced from government and NGA records.

Neither dataset contains any personal information about you or any other individual user.

Payment and App Store

Oathcard costs $5.99 as a one-time purchase. Payment is processed through Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android).

  • Apple App Store: Apple handles all payment processing and receipt management. We receive only a receipt confirmation; no credit card or payment details are shared with us.
  • Google Play: Google handles all payment processing. We receive only receipt and license-check information; no payment details are shared with us.

We do not store or process your payment information. That data is handled exclusively by Apple and Google according to their own privacy policies.

Device Permissions

Oathcard requests zero device permissions. The app does not ask for:

  • Microphone or audio access
  • Camera access
  • Location data
  • Contacts or calendar access
  • Photo library access
  • Health or fitness data

Children's Privacy

Oathcard does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children. Because we collect zero data from all users, there is no special handling of children's information.

Third-Party Services

Oathcard uses zero third-party services, analytics platforms, or external libraries that collect data. The app is built with no external dependencies.

Your Quiz Results and Study History

Your quiz scores, missed questions, and study progress are stored only on your device. They are never sent to us, to Apple, to Google, or to any other server. This data is completely yours and is accessible to you only.

Data Deletion

All of your app data (quiz history, score records, saved progress) is deleted when you uninstall the app. No data about you remains on our servers because we have no servers and collect no data.

How We Protect Your Offline Data

Since Oathcard runs offline with zero external communication:

  • Your device's standard encryption (iOS File Protection, Android encryption) protects your quiz history
  • Your data is only as secure as your device's physical security
  • We have no ability to access, sell, or share your quiz results

Questions or Concerns?

If you have questions about this privacy policy or about Oathcard's handling of data, you can reach us at support@8086.llc.

Changes to This Policy

This policy describes Oathcard's approach as of its launch in 2026. If the app ever collects data in the future (for example, in a new version), this policy will be updated to reflect that change clearly, and you will be notified.
